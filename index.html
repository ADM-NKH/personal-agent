Personal Agent

A private assistant used by one person, Adam Nikhamovsky.

Personal Agent runs on Adam's own computer. It reads his WhatsApp messages to notice promises, questions and agreed meetings, reminds him about them, and keeps his Google Calendar up to date (meetings he agreed to and his gym workouts).

It is not a public product: nobody else can sign in to it or use it.
